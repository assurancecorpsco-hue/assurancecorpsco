UAE Corporate Tax Audit Readiness: Records, Reconciliations and FTA Review Checklist for Dubai Businesses

Dubai businesses must prepare more than a Corporate Tax return for an FTA review. They must show where their reported figures came from. Each important amount should match accounting records and supporting documents.

UAE Corporate Tax law generally requires businesses to keep relevant records for seven years after the end of the relevant tax period, subject to applicable rules.

How to Complete a Corporate Tax Audit Readiness Review in 30 Days

Phase Main activities Deliverable

Days 13 to 20 Review tax adjustments, reliefs and related party positions. Risk and exception register
